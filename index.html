Privacy Policy — Kontrios

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This privacy policy describes how Nemahun21 collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Kontrios mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and password (stored by our authentication provider in hashed form).
  • Profile information: display name, optional profile photo, optional bio that you choose to provide.
  • App activity: quiz scores, level progress, streaks, badges, and content reactions. Used to show your progress and rankings on in-app leaderboards.
  • Device token: a push notification token issued by OneSignal, used to deliver notifications you have opted in to.
  • Diagnostic data: crash logs and basic performance information, kept only as long as needed to fix issues.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the App's features: quizzes, leaderboards, progress tracking.
  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To send notifications about daily challenges, only if you opted in.
  • To improve the App and fix bugs.

3. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

We process your data based on your consent, performance of the contract between you and us, and our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the App.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with these service providers:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage. https://supabase.com/privacy
  • OneSignal — push notifications. https://onesignal.com/privacy_policy
  • Google Play Services — app distribution and updates. https://policies.google.com/privacy

We may also disclose data if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where required by law.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at nemahun21@gmail.com.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on encrypted servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may store data outside your country. Where required, they rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: nemahun21@gmail.com
